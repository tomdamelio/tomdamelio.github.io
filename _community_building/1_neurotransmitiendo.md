My career in psychology and neuroscience has always been linked to my passion for effective communication. This led me to co-found [NeuroTransmitiendo](https://neurotransmitiendo.org/?lang=en), a Latin American nonprofit for neuroscience communication and education in Spanish.

In 2019, together with a team of colleagues, we identified a critical gap in the representation and communication of topics related to human behavior, neuroscience, and mental health in our region. Far too often, these topics were either misunderstood or trivialized, resulting in a landscape full of misinformation.

Our mission is to provide accurate, in-depth and accessible information about human behavior, neuroscience and mental health. The initiative grew quickly into one of the largest Spanish-speaking communities dedicated to neuroscience and mental health, with **nearly 40,000 followers** on social media and a team of **more than 30 volunteers**. We debunk common myths about mental health, including key issues such as suicide, anxiety and depression.

To achieve these goals, we create content on [Instagram](https://www.instagram.com/neurotransmitiendo/) through informative posts and use our [podcast](https://open.spotify.com/show/4h97z3s1rTwM2gKH8bYd1J) to interview renowned neuroscientists from across Latin America. I contribute to the podcast as an interviewer and general producer, delving into a wide range of topics in the field of neuroscience.

Since then the organization has grown well beyond communication. Across our courses and the **Diploma Program in Cognitive Neuroscience**, which I created and directed and which is now in its sixth edition, **more than 700 students** have trained with us, about half of the diploma students on scholarships. The diploma is taught by faculty from leading international universities. I also founded and organized the **first Latin American Conference of Cognitive Neuroscience** in 2025, now in its second edition, which across both years has drawn over 400 attendees and more than 100 posters.

We also offer low-cost psychotherapy in Argentina, take part in Brain Awareness Week every year, and work with **more than 100 partners**, including universities and other nonprofits across the region.

I have served on the Board of Directors since the organization was founded, with responsibilities in nonprofit governance, strategic planning and organizational development.
