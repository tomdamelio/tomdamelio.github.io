At [Purple Gaze](https://purplegaze.io/), my work as a data scientist revolves around improving eye-tracking technology and its application to brain research.

My primary responsibility is to develop a data analysis pipeline tailored to eye-tracking data that significantly improves researchers' ability to derive nuanced insights from complex eye movement patterns.

As a leader in experimental design at Purple Gaze, I have developed and implemented novel protocols that utilize eye-tracking technology to detect markers indicative of neurodevelopmental and neurodegenerative disorders, thereby maximizing the potential impact of our technology. These protocols and the associated data analysis pipeline are part of our continuous efforts to contribute to the field of brain research, and they are available for review on the [Purple Gaze GitHub repository](https://github.com/purple-gaze/experiments-gallery).
